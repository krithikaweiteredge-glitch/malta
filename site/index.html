II.

Order of Celebrations

Every celebration is written for the family it belongs to. Here is one way three days on the island can unfold.

The First Day · Late afternoon

Mehndi on the Bastions

Cushioned lounges among the old cannons, marigold and rose in the sea breeze, the Grand Harbour turning gold as the henna sets.

The First Day · Until late

Sangeet under the Lights

A live orchestra, full concert lighting and a dance floor that does not empty until the small hours.

The Second Day · Moonrise

Pheras beneath the Moon

White-flowered mandaps on a seafront terrace, with nothing behind them but the Mediterranean.

The Third Day · Evening

Wonderland at Palazzo Parisio

Living statues, masked performers and a banquet in gilded salons: the farewell that becomes family legend.

Finale

The Cannon Salute

A private firing of the Saluting Battery over the Grand Harbour. Some endings deserve to be heard across a city.
